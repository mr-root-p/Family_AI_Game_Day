How to play

1️⃣
Choose a game
Football is required. Word Chain and Photo Hunt are our original games.
2️⃣
Add players & teams
Type names and split them into Team A and Team B.
3️⃣
Play for real
Football: upload or record a video, pick team colours and mark the goals. Word Chain: press “Start listening” and speak. Photo Hunt: photograph each item.
4️⃣
Let the AI judge
Review what the AI found and why, then press “Apply AI results”. Every report is signed so the score can’t be changed.
⌨️
Shortcuts
Space timer · S skip · U undo · E end · Enter apply · ? help
💡
Tips
Use Chrome and allow the camera and microphone. Film football from a fixed side angle with a big, bright ball and different shirt colours.
